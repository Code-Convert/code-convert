# Code & Convert - Content Strategy Document

## Content Architecture Overview

This document outlines the content structure, copy requirements, and sections for each page in the Code & Convert website.

## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Core Brand Positioning
- **Primary Message**: "We Build, Market & Scale Digital Brands"
- **Value Proposition**: Elite web architecture designed to maximize conversion rates through data-backed design and psychological triggers
- **Tone**: Professional, confident, results-driven, technical expertise with business impact focus
- **Target Audience**: Business owners, marketing directors, e-commerce brands looking for serious growth

### Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Performance-Driven**: Everything is measured and optimized for conversions
2. **Technical Excellence**: Full-stack capabilities with modern tech stack
3. **Business Results**: Focus on ROI, revenue growth, and measurable outcomes
4. **Data-Backed**: Decisions driven by analytics and testing, not opinions

## Content Creation Guidelines

### Writing Style
- **Tone**: Professional but approachable, confident without being arrogant
- **Voice**: Expert advisor, results-focused, data-driven
- **Length**: Vary based on content type (case studies longer, service pages concise)
- **Structure**: Use clear headings, bullet points, and scannable format

### SEO Considerations
- Target keywords for each page
- Meta descriptions under 160 characters
- Header tag hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)
- Internal linking strategy
- Image alt text optimization

### Call-to-Action Strategy
- **Primary CTA**: "Book 1:1 Strategy Call"
- **Secondary CTA**: "View Case Studies" / "Get Free Audit"
- **Email CTA**: Newsletter signup
- **Social CTA**: Follow on social media

### Metrics to Highlight
- Conversion rate improvements (%)
- Revenue increases ($)
- Traffic growth (%)
- Load time improvements (seconds)
- User engagement metrics

This content structure provides a comprehensive foundation for all pages while maintaining the high-conversion, results-focused messaging that aligns with your brand positioning.
